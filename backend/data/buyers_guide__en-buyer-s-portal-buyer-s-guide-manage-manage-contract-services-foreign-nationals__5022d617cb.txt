Services of foreign nationals
In performing a contract, contractors may wish to temporarily use employees who are foreign nationals. The responsibility to determine eligibility for entering Canada lies with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.
For United States nationals, employment authorization can be applied for at the point of entry. For all other nationals, authorization must be obtained prior to entry. Applicants should contact the nearest Canadian embassy or consulate for the necessary documentation and authorizations.
Canadian citizens living abroad retain the right to work in Canada without restrictions.
Emergency service requirements
In cases of emergency services requirements, the client or buyer must provide the contractor with written notice detailing the circumstances of the emergency. In certain cases, this information may also be relayed via telephone to relevant immigration authorities to expedite processing.
