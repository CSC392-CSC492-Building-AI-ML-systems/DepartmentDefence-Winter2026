Insurance
Include applicable insurance clause(s) in the solicitation document and resulting contract that are appropriate for your procurement.
Insurance of government-owned or leased vehicles
The following tables provide additional information on the insurance of government-owned or leased vehicles.
Government-owned vehicles
self-underwriting option
third party liability and collision: commercial insurance
damage to vehicle: self-underwriting option
Other vehicles, including those leased by the government
comprehensive commercial insurance, including collision and third party liability
self-underwrite the deductible
purchase additional commercial insurance to cover third party liability and collision for the US risks
self-underwrite the deductibles
self-underwrite except if provincial legislation applies
self-underwrite any damage to government vehicle
short-term
• use commercial insurance coverage (third party liability and collision for the US risks) administered by the Real Property and Commercial Acquisitions Sector of PSP
Insurance of government-owned equipment
For government-owned equipment that will be operated by government employees, use the self-underwriting option for any loss or damage. For government-owned equipment that will be leased to a contractor, refer to the following table.
equipment floater insurance or equivalent coverage is required for any loss or damage to the equipment
self-underwriting option covers any loss or damage while operated by government employees
the contractor's insurance must cover any loss or damage to the equipment because it’s in the care, custody and control of the contractor
Insurance of equipment leased from a dealer
For equipment leased from a dealer, refer to the following table.
use the self-underwriting option to cover any loss or damage to the equipment
the contractor’s insurance must cover any loss or damage to the equipment
