Normal minimum solicitation periods
Regardless of minimum solicitation period requirements, offerors must always be given enough time to prepare and submit compliant offers. The following must be considered when determining the solicitation period for a procurement:
the nature and complexity of the procurement
the extent of subcontracting anticipated
the time necessary for transmitting tenders by non-electronic means
Minimum solicitation periods for procurements
May be less than 15 calendar days for efficiency and cost effectiveness:
Goods: below $25K (including taxes)
Construction: below $40K (including taxes)
Services: below $40K (including taxes)
15 calendar days if the Notice of Proposed Procurement (NPP) and tender documents are published at the same time electronically
13 calendar days if 15 calendar days is impracticable
10 calendar days if offers are accepted electronically
Note: If the NPP and tender documents are not published at the same time electronically, the guidance for non-commercial goods and/or services below applies.
40 calendar days, which can be reduced by 5 days, to no less than 25 days, for each of the following conditions:
the NPP is published electronically
all tender documents are available electronically from the NPP date of publication
the procuring entity accepts tenders electronically
Note: If using limited tendering, the solicitation period should be at least 15 calendar days.
