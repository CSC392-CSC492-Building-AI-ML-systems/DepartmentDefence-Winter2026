Notice of proposed procurement
You, the buyer, post a Notice of Proposed Procurement (NPP) on the Government Electronic Tendering Service (GETS) to advertise a competitive procurement opportunity to businesses. The NPP summarizes the solicitation and must include:
sufficient information for businesses to determine their interest and their ability to fulfill the requirements
a list of the applicable agreements, trade or otherwise
whether Canadian content restrictions apply
whether the requirement has been set aside under the Procurement Strategy for Indigenous Business
whether a National Security Exception applies
whether additional material will be posted on GETS or made available separately
The NPP should advise businesses of their option to request a debriefing. Any other notices (for example, newspapers) should contain the same statement.
When entering the point of delivery information on the NPP for a standing offer or supply arrangement, you must select only those provinces or territories where potential deliveries may occur.
Many procurement units have created templates to assist you in developing NPP s. You should consult your manager to obtain such templates.
