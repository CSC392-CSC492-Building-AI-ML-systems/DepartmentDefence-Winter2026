Contacting businesses directly during the solicitation period
Sometimes commodity or market knowledge may lead a buyer to conclude that businesses supplying a good or service will not see or respond to a solicitation of offers if it appears only on the Government Electronic Tendering Service (GETS). To stimulate effective competition and seek best value for Canadian taxpayers, you may notify all such known businesses that the solicitation opportunity has been posted. This communication must only occur after the Notice of Proposed Procurement (NPP) has been posted on the GETS . You should contact businesses as soon as possible afterwards so that they do not lose time.
Prompt and non-preferential communication
Written communications will show that all businesses had access to the same information at the same time.
The content shared must be limited to providing:
only brief information about the goods or services being procured
appropriate references (for example reference number, source ID, solicitation number)
only include information that is available on GETS
The recommended approach for notifying businesses is to provide them with a copy of the NPP .
Documentation
You must document your file with the date and the names of businesses contacted.
