How to reissue a solicitation
You must include a clause about the reissuance of the solicitation of offers at the beginning of the reissued solicitation and NPP documents.
If canceling and reissuing a solicitation that is subject to the international trade agreements and the Canadian Free Trade Agreement (CFTA), you should publish a new NPP . If no compliant offers were received under the original competitive solicitation, and the requirement is not being changed significantly, you may send the reissued solicitation directly to businesses without publishing a new NPP . However, when following this approach, it is strongly recommended that you:
consider reposting the NPP to enhance openness and transparency
state in the NPP that some businesses will be invited directly
