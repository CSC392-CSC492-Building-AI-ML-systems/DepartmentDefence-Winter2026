# Adversarial benchmark questions for policy RAG stress-testing.
# One question per line; comments are ignored.
# See docs/EVALUATION.md for guidance on when to use this set.
We are issuing a non-defence RFP under an existing supply arrangement renewal, and a supplier from a non-trade-partner country submits an offer after closing with evidence of system delay; under reciprocal procurement and late-offer rules, is the supplier eligible, can the offer be considered, and what exact file documentation and approvals are mandatory before award?
During an RFSA renewal cycle, can reciprocal procurement clauses be applied to solicitations issued under the existing SA before the renewal is completed, and what wording should appear in the RFSA notice versus individual RFPs?
A supplier submits a reciprocal procurement declaration after closing but within two business days of notification; if their address appears inaccurate, what verification steps and potential remedies are allowed before contract award?
For a non-defence procurement where a best value exception and public interest exception are both being considered under reciprocal procurement, who must approve each exception in writing and what has to be included in the procurement file?
If a buyer uses ACAN because only one supplier seems capable, but the requirement is also under a supply arrangement context, what justifications are mandatory for ACAN and which parts would not apply to a standard competitive SA call-up?
When handling a late offer, what exact evidence is acceptable to prove timely submission, which late offers are returned, and what records must be retained in the file?
If the offer validity period will expire before evaluation completes, what extension process is allowed, what happens if some offerors refuse, and what is the consequence of awarding after expiry?
For supply arrangements, how do trade agreement thresholds change obligations at RFSA stage versus individual contracts under the SA, including when a notice of proposed procurement is required?
If a supplier is found ineligible or suspended during pre-award checks, when can a public interest exception be invoked, who needs to be escalated/notified, and what records are required?
Under the Buy Canadian policy instruments, when exceptions are invoked by a common service provider on behalf of multiple departments, what approvals and supporting documentation are required prior to solicitation?
For pressing emergency contracting under the TBS directive, what conditions must be met before using emergency limits, and what reporting/documentation must be produced after award?
If reciprocal procurement allows non-trading-partner suppliers in certain scenarios but Buy Canadian supplier policy says non-trading-partner offers are ineligible in others, how should a buyer determine which rule applies and what documentation is needed to justify the path taken?
When a requirement is potentially affected by both trade agreements and modern treaty (CLCA) obligations, what should be documented to justify applicability decisions and rights of first refusal handling?
In an integrity verification workflow before award, what confirmations must be retained in the procurement file, and what happens if additional information requested by IVS is not provided by the supplier within the timeline?
