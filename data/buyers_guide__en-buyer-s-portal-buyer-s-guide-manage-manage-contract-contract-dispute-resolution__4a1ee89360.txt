Contract dispute resolution
When managing contracts, disputes can occur between the involved parties. Disputes need to be addressed quickly and efficiently so that all parties can meet their contractual obligations. Maintaining accurate records is crucial for clarification, audits or possible termination of contracts.
If you cannot resolve a contract dispute, the issue should be escalated to your immediate supervisor and you should consult Legal Services to protect the interests of Canada.
Handling conflicts with contractors
Resolve any conflicts with contractors as soon as they arise. If unresolved, involve a senior level authority to mediate and help settle the dispute.
Dispute Management
Should escalation to a senior authority not resolve the dispute, you should inform the contractor in writing about the alternative dispute resolution (ADR) services available through the Office of the Procurement Ombud (OPO).
Any party to a contract can request assistance from OPO for dispute management. Upon request for ADR services, the OPO program will inform all parties involved and assess their interest in the process. If the parties agree, the ADR process will commence.
It's important to note that if the ADR process does not resolve the dispute, it does not affect the parties’ rights.
