Types of basis of payment
There are four main types of basis of payment used in contracts and it is possible to use multiple types in one contract. These include:
fixed price competitive
fixed price non-competitive
fixed price subject to economic price adjustments
fixed time/unit rate with or without an economic price adjustment
no fee
fixed fee
target cost / incentive fee
fee based on actual costs
provisional price
When choosing the types of basis of payment to use for a contract there are many items to consider, including:
clarity of the scope and requirements
market forces
contract duration
price validation
contractor readiness
applicability of incentives
Note : A contract using a fixed time/unit rate or a cost reimbursable basis of payment must include either a ceiling price or a limitation of expenditure.
