Delivery terms
Delivery terms are essential in defining how goods are transported and delivered to the client. These terms outline the responsibilities regarding the consignment and delivery of goods.
Transportation costs
For most requirements with an estimated expenditure of $25,000 or more, including applicable taxes, with known delivery points, offers should be solicited on the basis of Free On Board (FOB) destination.
For unknown delivery points, offers should be solicited on the basis of FOB origin only. When Incoterms are used, you must ensure that businesses understand the differences in the acronyms used particularly those that use the same letters: “ FOB ” in incoterms means “Free On-Board”, not “Freight on Board”.
The Incoterms 2000 term " FCA Free Carrier (named place)" must be used in:
all Department of National Defence (DND) sole source contracts
all repair and overhaul contracts where transportation is not part of competitive offer, and
United States Foreign Military Sales (FMS) contracts
DND will manage the inbound logistics, including coordination, arrangement and payment for all inbound transportation. For these contracts, the contractor must deliver these goods "FCA Free Carrier" and the named place will always be the contractor's facility, unless DND specifies otherwise.
The buyer must include the applicable clauses in the contract to direct the contractor to obtain shipping instructions from DND and how to do so.
If the contractor is not located in Canada, and the goods are to be imported into Canada by DND , the buyer must include the applicable clauses into the contract.
