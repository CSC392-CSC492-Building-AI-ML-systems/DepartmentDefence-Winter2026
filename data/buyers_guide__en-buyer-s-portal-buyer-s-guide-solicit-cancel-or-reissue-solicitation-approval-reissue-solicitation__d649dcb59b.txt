Approval to reissue a solicitation
You will need to obtain approval from the appropriate authority before reissuing a solicitation. To determine the appropriate approval authority, buyers must first assess whether there is a significant change to the requirement, procurement strategy or risk level.
Significant change to the requirement, strategy or risk level
If you plan to reissue a solicitation because of a significant change to the requirement, you must assess whether the change will affect the procurement strategy or risk level.
If the procurement strategy or risk level will change, a Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) buyer must conduct a new procurement risk assessment. For example, if the original strategy was to compete and the revised strategy is to sole source, then you, as a PSPC buyer, must perform a new procurement risk assessment to determine if the strategy change impacts the risks already identified. The procurement risk assessment will also identify the appropriate level of contract entry approval based on the revised identified risks.
A PSPC buyer must:
include details about the new solicitation in the approval document
obtain approval for the revised procurement strategy even if the risk assessment indicates the same approval level as was originally sought
No change to the requirement or strategy
Before reissuing a solicitation with no change to the requirement or procurement strategy, if you are a PSPC buyer you must obtain approval from the appropriate approval authority. They are detailed below:
complexity level 1: buyer
complexity level 2: manager
complexity level 3: manager
complexity level 4: director or regional director
complexity level 5: director general or regional director general
