Standing offers and supply arrangements
The methods outlined below are used to manage standing offers and supply arrangements.

Managing a proportional basis of selection for a standing offer
When a proportional basis of selection is used to select the contractor among multiple standing offers, you must ensure that identified users are aware of their obligation to follow the predetermined work distributions specified in the standing offers.
The offeror whose actual work received is furthest below the predetermined ideal percentage detailed in the standing offer will be selected for the next call-up. A spreadsheet may be used to track all call-ups and to maintain a cumulative total for each offeror.
Call-ups against standing offers with no ranking methodology
When multiple standing offers are available without a ranking, the client must justify each call-up.
Offerors' reporting obligations
You must ensure that offerors submit reports as required in the standing offer or supply arrangement. Typically, offerors must report on a quarterly basis on their call-up or contract activities.
These reports may include, but are not limited to:
standing offer or supply arrangement number
business name
reporting period
each call-up or contract number, including any amendments
client department
contracting authority
date of call-up or contract
period of call-up or contract
line items acquired or services provided
total value of each call-up or contract, including applicable taxes
Client reporting
You must also ensure that clients promptly fulfill their reporting responsibilities as identified users of the standing offer or supply arrangement. These reports may require more detail than those from offerors and include information on:
the solicitation of offers
the responses from offerors
the selection of successful offerors
