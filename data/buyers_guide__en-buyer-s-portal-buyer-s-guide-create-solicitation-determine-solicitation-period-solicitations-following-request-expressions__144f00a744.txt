Solicitations following a request for expressions of interest
If a solicitation that follows a request for expressions of interest is subject to one or more international trade agreements, the solicitation period may be reduced to 15 calendar days if a previous NPP was published at least 40 days, but no more than 12 months, before the publication of the new NPP . The previously published NPP must have contained the following elements for the solicitation period to be reduced:
a description of the procurement
the approximate final dates to submit offers or requests to participate
a request that businesses express their interest to the procuring entity
the address to obtain the procurement documents
as much of the information required for the new NPP , as is available
