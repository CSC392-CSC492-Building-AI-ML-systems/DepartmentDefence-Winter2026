## Delegation of authority guidelines

Delegated authority to enter into, sign and amend contracts must be aligned with the responsibilities assigned to each position. These authorities are documented through formal delegation instruments and are designed to ensure that no individual exercises authority beyond what is required for their role. Managers may adjust authority levels, within approved frameworks, to reflect operational needs and ensure effective procurement delivery.

### Delegation

The authority is valid when the incumbent’s schedule 3 form is approved and signed. The authority can be limited to a certain period of time (for example, during an acting period).

### Incumbent

This form documents the scope of the incumbent’s delegated approval and signing authority.
