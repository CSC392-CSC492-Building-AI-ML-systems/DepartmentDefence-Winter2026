## Buyer and client responsibilities

Contract management is a shared responsibility between you, the buyer, and the client. To ensure the integrity of the procurement process, both parties must understand and agree on the roles in managing the contract.

In addition to Treasury Board policies, sanctions may be applied to either a department or specific officials if procurement practices are found unacceptable. It is your responsibility to be aware of any sanctions involving a contract you are managing.

You must also adhere to Canada's obligations. If a responsibility lies with a client, you should ensure the client is aware of that obligation. A breach of Canada’s obligations could nullify a surety bond.

It is your responsibility to notify the contractor if the delivered goods or services do not meet the contractual requirements. Otherwise, any future claims by Canada may be jeopardized.
