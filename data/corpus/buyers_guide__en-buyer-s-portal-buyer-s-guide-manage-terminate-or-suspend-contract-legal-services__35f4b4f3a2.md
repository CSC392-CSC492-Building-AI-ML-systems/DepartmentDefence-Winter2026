## Legal Services

The following terminations must not be issued without a written legal opinion:

- all terminations for default
- terminations by mutual consent
- terminations for convenience

In seeking the opinion of legal services, you should submit the procurement file with a chronological index of the documents forming the basis for the termination request. A short note outlining the events leading to termination must also be included. Based on this information, legal services will render an opinion and advise as to the appropriate method of termination.
