## Tender notices and trade agreements

For procurements subject to one or more of Canada's trade agreements, you must adhere to the public advertisement and notification provisions specified within these agreements. This includes any mini-tenders for procurements made against a supply arrangement.

### Key procedures and provisions

All procedures related to public advertisement and notification must comply with the terms outlined in the relevant trade agreements. Unless specific exceptions apply, competitive procurements covered by trade agreements must be advertised on Government Electronic Tendering Service (GETS). Following the posting of a notice, there may be circumstances where it is appropriate to contact potential businesses directly, as detailed in Communicate with offerors .

The provisions for public advertisement/notification are described in each of the trade agreements, including:

- Canada-European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA): Article 19.6: Notices
- World Trade Organization - Agreement on Government Procurement (WTO-AGP): Article VII: Notices
- Canadian Free Trade Agreement (CFTA): refer to “Tender Notices” article

The procedures to be followed are generally consistent for all of the agreements. In cases of inconsistency among agreements, you must choose the provisions that exemplify the highest standard of openness, such as opting for the longer of two solicitation periods. For detailed guidance on when trade agreements apply, refer to the relevant sections that outline the coverage under a trade agreement .
