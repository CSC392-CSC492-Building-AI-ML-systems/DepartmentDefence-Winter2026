## Communications during the solicitation period

To ensure the integrity of the competitive solicitation process, businesses must direct all communications about the solicitation only to the buyer identified in the solicitation of offers, not to the client or other government officials.

Buyers should avoid one-on-one contact with businesses during the solicitation period. All communications should be in writing, to the extent possible.
