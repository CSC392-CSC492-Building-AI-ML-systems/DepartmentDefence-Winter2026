Determining complexity Each requisition undergoes an assessment to determine the procurement's complexity level In order to direct the requirement through the appropriate process stream. Complexity levels range from 1 to 5. The manager is responsible for determining the complexity of a requirement and the selected complexity level must be documented on file, dated and signed. Any changes to the complexity level during the course of the procurement must be discussed with the manager, documented on file, explaining why the change was necessary.
