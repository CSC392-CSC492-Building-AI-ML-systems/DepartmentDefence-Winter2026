## Roles and accountabilities

In the peer review process, several key roles ensure a thorough and fair evaluation. Here’s a breakdown of the responsibilities for each role:

### Buyer

- maintains accountability for the procurement throughout the peer review process
- collaborates with the reviewer(s) to mutually decide on the time frame for conducting the peer review, not exceeding two business days
- attempts to resolve any issues that arise with the reviewer(s)
- escalates unresolved issues to one designated level up for resolution

### Reviewer

- conducts the peer review
- collaborates with the buyer to mutually decide on the time frame for conducting the peer review, not exceeding two business days
- attempts to resolve any issues that arise during the review with the buyer
- escalates unresolved issues to one designated level up for resolution
- initials and dates the evaluation report to attest that the review has occurred
