## Offer receipt

The method and location of bid receipt must be specified in the solicitation of offers.

Here are the acceptable methods for offer submission and their conditions:

- In writing and submitted to the specified receiving unit: applicable for all ($) dollar values

- In writing and submitted directly to the buyer: for offers below $25,000 for goods and $40,000 for construction and services, including all applicable taxes

- Verbal submission by telephone:
- for offers below $25,000 for goods and $40,000 for construction and services, including all applicable taxes
- for any ($) dollar value in cases of documented extreme urgency, the director's approval is mandatory

- On electronic media (e.g., DVD s, USB keys, CD s) and submitted to the specified receiving unit

- By facsimile

- Canada Post Corporation’s Connect service

In situations where a particular method of response is deemed not acceptable, you must clearly indicate this in the solicitation document and in the Notice of Proposed Procurement (NPP).
