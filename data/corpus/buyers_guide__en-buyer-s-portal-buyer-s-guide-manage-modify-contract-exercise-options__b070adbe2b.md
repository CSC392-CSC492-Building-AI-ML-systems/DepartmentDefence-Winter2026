## Exercise options

Before exercising a contract option, you must verify if the option was pre-approved. If the option was not pre-approved, you need to obtain approval from the appropriate authority before amending the contract. This ensures that the necessary oversight and due diligence are maintained.

Additionally, you should ensure that the option's pricing is still fair and reasonable. If market pricing for the goods or services has decreased significantly since the contract award, negotiating a lower price may be appropriate.
