## Revisions to standing offers

Standing offers are not contracts and so cannot be amended or assigned. Amendments to the terms or the duration are managed through a process of revisions. The key points regarding revisions are detailed below.

### Withdrawal notification

Standing offers typically have a provision that an offeror must provide 30 days written notice of its intent to withdraw its request for standing offer (RFSO). You would then issue a "Revision to the Standing Offer and Call-up Authority", to notify all the identified users and the offeror of the effective date of the withdrawal. Call-ups received by the offeror before the effective withdrawal date are legally binding and must be honored.

### Revision issuance

You can issue revisions to extend the period of the standing offer (SO) or to update any pertinent details within the standing offer.

### Offeror revisions

Offerors may revise their SO within the parameters set by the RFSO solicitation process. You would then issue a "Revision to the standing offer and call-up authority" to reflect the appropriate revisions.
