## Early engagement

By engaging with clients and offerors through early and ongoing consultation and dialogue, you are better situated to identify the complexities and risks associated with a client’s requirement. It enables the development of mitigation strategies that places all stakeholders in a better position for a successful procurement that meets the client’s needs.

You, can also engage early with industry through various methods, such as issuing letters of Interest (LOI), Requests for Information (RFI), conducting one-on-one consultations and organizing industry days.

### Resources to support early engagement

To ensure that you are successful initiating early engagement Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) offers various resources. The resources available to you are:

- Acquisitions Program Policy Suite (accessible only on the Government of Canada network) which provides:
- policies, directives
- guidelines on engagement, communication, governance, socio-economic objectives
- risk management support

- Quick reference resources
- Procurement Information Nuggets (accessible only on the Government of Canada network)
- guides on procurement issues, offering quick references.
