## Certifications

When certifications are provided in an offer, they are subject to verification by Canada throughout the period of the contract, standing offer or supply arrangement. If the contractor, offeror or business does not comply with any certification, or if any certification is found to be false, Canada may:

- terminate the contract for default
- set aside the standing offer or supply arrangement
- remove the business from the qualified business list

### Indigenous business certification

After a contract is awarded, the contractor's certification is audited to confirm its status as an Indigenous business and to ensure compliance with Indigenous content requirements throughout the contract.

Audits after contract award are typically done randomly. However, if an audit of the contractor's status as an Indigenous business is necessary, the group within Indigenous Services Canada (ISC) that supports Indigenous businesses may be contacted.

If the Indigenous business certification is declared invalid, or if the contractor does not qualify as an Indigenous business, civil or contractual measures may need to be taken. Legal services and ISC should be consulted for guidance on the next steps in this case.
