## Employer-employee relationship

Ensure that descriptions of the work or changes to the work do not promote the development of an employer-employee relationship. Clients must ensure that an employer-employee relationship does not develop during contract performance. For more context on how to avoid an employer-employee relationship, refer to the section on Employer-employee relationship .
