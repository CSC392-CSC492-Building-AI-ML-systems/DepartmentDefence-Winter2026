## Prepare for an evaluation

As the buyer, you are responsible for determining the completeness of an offer as specified in the solicitation of offers. You must ensure that all information required at offer closing is available and ready to be provided to the evaluators before starting the technical and financial evaluation.

For example, this means verifying:

- that certifications or securities required at offer closing are included
- offers are properly signed
- the offeror is identified (particularly important in the case of joint ventures)
- acceptance of the terms and conditions of the offer and resulting contract
- the ability to meet a clearly specified critical delivery schedule
- whether the offer is conditional (for example, limitation of liability)
- that all documents required to determine technical responsiveness have been submitted

Note : It is recommended that buyers create and use a check list of all requirements which can be used throughout the evaluation of each offer.
