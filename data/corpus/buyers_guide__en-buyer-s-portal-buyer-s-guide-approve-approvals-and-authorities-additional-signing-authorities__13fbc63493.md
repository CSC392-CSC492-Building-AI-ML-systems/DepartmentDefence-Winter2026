## Additional signing authorities

Signing authority for procurement instruments including the below must be exercised according to the contracting limits, including exceptional authorities as outlined. They include:

- purchase orders
- contracts
- standing offers
- supply arrangements
- formal agreements and arrangements
- stores and supply transfer orders
- written direction to the agency of record
- assignments
- go-ahead letters and messages
- letters of intent
- consents to subcontract
- termination for convenience notices
- amendments to any of the above

### Advance, milestone and progress payments

The authority for certification of advance, milestone and progress payment claims form PWGSC-TPSGC 1111 (Claim for Progress Payment) is a prerequisite for client certification pursuant to section 34 of the Financial Administration Act is as follows:

- Unlimited certification authority applies for incumbents of positions listed in the Table of equivalent positions within AP , with the exception of intern officers/trainees, senior purchasing assistants and procurement assistants.

Senior purchasing assistants have certification authority as follows:

- for contracts beyond their approval authority: claims up to $40,000 with the exception of a final claim or of a release of a holdback
- for contracts within approval authority: all claims

### Settlement and release documents

The authority to sign release and settlement documents (form PWGSC-TPSGC 9223-2 , for terminations on behalf of the Minister of PSPC is delegated as follows

- arising from a termination for convenience:
- DG , Procurement Support Services Sector, AP
- Director, Centralized Services Directorate, AP
- for other than a termination of convenience, PSPC buyers must consult with Finance Branch to determine the appropriate approval authority level

For assistance on terminations for convenience, consult the Specialized Services for Procurement Division (SSPD) .

### Contract security

The Contract Security Program has the authority to provide instructions to contractors concerning compliance with contract security requirements.
