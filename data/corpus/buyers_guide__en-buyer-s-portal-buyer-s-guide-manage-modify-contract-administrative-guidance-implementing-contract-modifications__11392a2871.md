## Administrative guidance for implementing contract amendments

The guidance detailed below will help you fulfill the administrative requirements for modifying a contract.

- Follow the original contract format and identify all modifications
- Consolidate multiple changes into a single amendment whenever possible to minimize administrative costs
- Ensure amendments receive the same distribution as the original contract to maintain transparency and accurate records
