## Contract close-out activities

When closing out a contract, you, the buyer, must ensure all contract activities are completed. A contract is fully completed when all of the following are complete:

- the required deliverables have been received as specified in the contract
- the client has accepted the deliverables and the invoice has been paid according to the contract price and basis of payment
- there are no unresolved work deficiencies, changes, payments, or claims requiring formal release action

### Administrative closure

You must also ensure administrative closure of the file is performed in line with established procedures. This includes reporting, returning government-furnished equipment, and disposing of surplus material and assets. Additionally, the procurement file must be accurately documented and archived in accordance with records management procedures .
