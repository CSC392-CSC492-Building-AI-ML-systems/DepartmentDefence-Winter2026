## Considerations for amendments

When approval is required to issue an amendment, you must complete a Procurement Risk Assessment for Amendments (PRAA).

See the Acquisition Program Risk Assessment Framework (accessible only on the Government of Canada network [for PSPC only]) for more information on the Risk Assessment Framework.
