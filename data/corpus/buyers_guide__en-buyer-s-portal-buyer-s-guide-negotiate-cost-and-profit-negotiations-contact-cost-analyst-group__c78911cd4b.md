## Contact the Price Advisory Group

You may engage with subject matter experts, including price advisors for procurements with a total estimated value of $7,500,000 or more.

You may also consult price advisors on any matter where financial advice is deemed helpful.

The Price Advisory Group provides the following services and more:

- advice on matters related to pricing considerations and validation
- mitigation of pricing risks associated with complex or negotiated procurements
- contract cost-price analysis
- negotiations of costing rates
- development of basis of payment strategies to achieve best value

Contact the Price Advisory Group
