## Canadian General Standards Board

The Canadian General Standards Board (CGSB) is an organization within Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC), accredited by the Standards Council of Canada. CGSB offers standards development and conformity assessment services in support of the economic, regulatory, procurement, health, safety and environmental interests of our stakeholders, including government, industry and consumers.

For certain procurements, offerors may be required to meet CGSB standards and to reference this compliance in their offers and resulting contracts. CGSB also provides expertise and information on:

- national and international standardization
- the assessment of the suitability of standards
- quality and environmental management systems registration
- qualification/certification of listing programs for specific products and services

By visiting the Canadian General Standards Board website, government organizations, contractors and the general public can view:

- the CGSB catalogue of approximately 60 standards
- information on the listing programs
- documentation required to apply for a listing

### Initiating new standards, specifications or listings

When a need for a new standard, specification or listing is identified, and nothing suitable is under development, you should suggest that that the client contact CGSB . If the need is specific to a single client, sector or region, they may establish a qualification program specific to them. The client, sector or region should establish procedures that promote open competition and equal opportunity for all businesses. If criteria other than technical performance are applied, the distribution of listings should be restricted. Any distributed listings should clearly state the qualification criteria.
