# Buy Canadian Policy

On September 5, 2025, the Government of Canada announced new measures to protect, build, and transform Canadian strategic industries. Central to this plan is the introduction of a new Buy Canadian Policy suite, designed to help make Canada’s economy stronger, more resilient to global shocks, and better able to support Canadian workers and businesses.

The Government of Canada is strengthening opportunities for Canadian businesses in federal procurement through the following measures:

1. Buy Canadian Procurement Policy Framework . The Framework sets the foundation for implementing Canadian preference measures across federal procurement. Its purpose is to strengthen Canada’s economic resilience by prioritizing Canadian materials, suppliers, and content in government purchasing, while supporting reciprocal procurement with trusted trade partners and improving access for Canadian small and medium businesses (SMBs).
2. Policy on Prioritizing Canadian Materials in Federal Procurements . The Government of Canada is taking new steps to ensure federal procurement spending supports Canadian jobs and industries. This policy requires the use of Canadian-produced materials in major federal construction and defence projects. It begins with three pillars of Canada’s industrial base: steel, aluminum, and wood products, and applies defined thresholds for each material. The policy also provides flexibility to expand to other key materials, as needed, to strengthen domestic supply chains and support Canadian industry.
3. Policy on Prioritizing Canadian Suppliers and Canadian Content in Strategic Federal Procurements . The Government of Canada is taking action to ensure federal procurement spending delivers for Canadians. New measures under this policy encourage the participation of Canadian suppliers, and incentivize the use of Canadian Content in Strategic Federal Procurements so that every dollar spent by the federal government supports Canadian workers, businesses, and communities. This approach will strengthen our supply chains, create good jobs across the country, diversify our economy, and ensure that federal investments benefit Canadian industries and workers even when foreign suppliers are involved.
4. Interim Policy on Reciprocal Procurement . The Government of Canada is putting fairness and reciprocity at the heart of federal contracting. Under this policy, Canadian suppliers and those from countries that provide Canada with equivalent procurement market access through trade agreements are prioritized for federal contracts. By Spring 2026, this policy will be fully implemented, ensuring that non-defence contracts are only awarded to Canadian suppliers, goods, and services, or to those from trusted partners with reciprocal procurement market access. This approach protects and supports Canadian workers and businesses, strengthens supply chains, and ensures that federal procurement spending truly benefits Canada’s economy.
5. Supporting Small and Medium Businesses . A new Small and Medium Business Procurement Program will create tailored streams for SMBs , provide dedicated support to help them navigate the federal system, and ensure they can compete effectively for federal contracts.
