The Buyer's Guide offers information and guidance on procurement policies and processes.

Plan your procurement by strategizing, mobilizing stakeholders, and supporting your customer.

Get information on approval authorities and processes for contracts, standing offers, and supply arrangements.

Prepare your solicitation document and tender notice.

## Solicit: from launch to closure

Release and manage your solicitation document.

## Receive and evaluate

Negotiate with offerors before awarding a contract, applying cost and profit principles as needed.

## Award

Award the contract, communicate the results, and hold debrief and feedback sessions.

## Manage

Manage the contract and payments, modify, suspend, or terminate the contract if necessary, and close it.
