## How to negotiate

It is your responsibility to conduct all negotiations. If the negotiations are of a technical nature, you will work with the client.

During negotiations:

- all offerors must be treated fairly and impartially
- confidentiality of each offeror's negotiating position must be assured

Note: Negotiations should not become an auction where offerors continuously enhance their offers based on insights into their competitors' positions.

Upon conclusion, you must include a negotiation report in the procurement file. At a minimum, the report must describe the following elements: date and participants; a summary of the discussions; any changes to the offer; the agreements that were reached; and the final decision.
