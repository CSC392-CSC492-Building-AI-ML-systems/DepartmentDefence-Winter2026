Procedures for posting solicitation documents on the Government Electronic Tendering Service Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) buyers use the Electronic Procurement Solution (EPS) to create their solicitations of offers for each publicly advertised competitive solicitation that are then posted on the Government Electronic Tendering Service (GETS). When posting on GETS , you must ensure that the notice and all related solicitation documents, including attachments, are accurate, complete and successfully posted. Buyers should check for any discrepancies on GETS the day after issuing a notice. You are responsible for making any required corrections to the solicitation of offers, with the assistance of the CanadaBuys Service Desk .
