Communicating the method of selection A Canadian International Trade Tribunal (CITT) ruling emphasizes the importance of clearly communicating the method of selection in the solicitation documents. Even if it awarding contracts to the lowest responsive offeror is a recognized practice, you must still state the method of selection. If there is an intent to rely on a publicly available policy, you must incorporate it into the solicitation documents to avoid any ambiguity.
