## Insurance

Include applicable insurance clause(s) in the solicitation document and resulting contract that are appropriate for your procurement.

### Insurance of government-owned or leased vehicles

The following tables provide additional information on the insurance of government-owned or leased vehicles.

Government-owned vehicles

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country | Period | Policy requirement |
| Canada | long-term | self-underwriting option |
| United States (US) | long-term, or for trips to US | third party liability and collision: commercial insurance damage to vehicle: self-underwriting option |

Other vehicles, including those leased by the government

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type of vehicle | Country | Term of lease | Policy requirement |
| executive | Canada | long-term or short-term | comprehensive commercial insurance, including collision and third party liability self-underwrite the deductible |
| executive | US | long-term | comprehensive commercial insurance, including collision and third party liability self-underwrite the deductible |
| executive | US | short-term | purchase additional commercial insurance to cover third party liability and collision for the US risks self-underwrite the deductibles |
| non-executive | Canada | long-term | self-underwrite except if provincial legislation applies |
| non-executive | Canada | short-term | comprehensive commercial insurance, including collision and third party liability self-underwrite the deductible |
| non-executive | US | long-term | purchase additional commercial insurance to cover third party liability and collision for the US risks self-underwrite any damage to government vehicle |
| non-executive | US | short-term | • use commercial insurance coverage (third party liability and collision for the US risks) administered by the Real Property and Commercial Acquisitions Sector of PSP self-underwrite the deductible |

### Insurance of government-owned equipment

For government-owned equipment that will be operated by government employees, use the self-underwriting option for any loss or damage. For government-owned equipment that will be leased to a contractor, refer to the following table.

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Who will control the work of the operator or driver? | Policy requirement |
| there is no operator or driver | equipment floater insurance or equivalent coverage is required for any loss or damage to the equipment |
| government | self-underwriting option covers any loss or damage while operated by government employees the contractor’s insurance must cover any loss or damage to the equipment while it’s under the contractor's care, custody and control |
| contractor | the contractor's insurance must cover any loss or damage to the equipment because it’s in the care, custody and control of the contractor |

### Insurance of equipment leased from a dealer

For equipment leased from a dealer, refer to the following table.

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who employs the operator or driver? | Who will control the work of the operator or driver? | Policy requirement |
| government | government | use the self-underwriting option to cover any loss or damage to the equipment |
| government | government (employer-employee relationship) | use the self-underwriting option to cover any loss or damage to the equipment |
| contractor | dealer | the contractor’s insurance must cover any loss or damage to the equipment |
