## Pricing methods

Prices for goods and services can be established through various methods:

- market pricing
- commercial pricing
- negotiated pricing

To establish a commercial price, obtain sufficient price support to validate the offeror’s price. Negotiated pricing is used when a contract requires any type of price negotiations, such as for:

- a non-competitive contract
- after a competitive contract has been awarded for aspects not included in the initial financial bids (for example, contract amendments)
- the use of incentives involving costs

See Cost and profit negotiations for more information on pricing methods.
