## When to cancel or reissue a solicitation

You may cancel and reissue a solicitation if:

- a contract, standing offer, or supply arrangement hasn’t been issued and:
- a significant change has occurred in the requirement, or
- the acceptance period for the offer has expired
- one of the following applies, in which case the trade agreements allow the limited tendering procedure to be applied if the solicitation is reissued:
- no offers are compliant
- no offers represent fair value
- no offers were received in response to a competitive solicitation

You must cancel a competitive solicitation if no compliant offers are received.

A reminder that you are responsible for internal distribution of solicitations and updates within Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) and to the client.

Anytime a solicitation is cancelled or reissued, you must document the procurement file accordingly, including the rationale for the decision.
