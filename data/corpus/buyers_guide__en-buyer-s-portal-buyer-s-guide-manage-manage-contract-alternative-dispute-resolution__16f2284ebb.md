## Alternative dispute resolution

You and a contractor can access Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) services available through the Office of the Procurement Ombud (OPO). These services can help resolve contractual disputes related to the interpretation or application of terms and conditions of a federal contract.

OPO ’s ADR services:

- are available to most federal departments and agencies
- are facilitated by certified mediators
- apply to contractual disputes relating to the interpretation or application of the terms and conditions of a contract
- are only conducted when both parties voluntarily agree to participate

If you're a buyer at Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) and want to use ADR services, you need director-level approval before submitting a request to OPO .

To learn more or submit a request, Please visit: Dispute resolution services - Office of the Procurement Ombud .
