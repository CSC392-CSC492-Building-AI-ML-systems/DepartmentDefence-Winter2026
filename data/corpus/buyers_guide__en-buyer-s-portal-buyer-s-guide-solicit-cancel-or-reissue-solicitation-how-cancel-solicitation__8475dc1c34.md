## How to cancel a solicitation

If a solicitation is cancelled before the closing date, you must:

- issue a cancellation notice on the Government Electronic Tendering Service (GETS), which will mark the notice of proposed procurement (NPP) as ‘cancelled’ on GETS
- notify the Offer Receiving Unit (ORU) of the cancellation and provide disposal instructions for any responses to the original solicitation

If a solicitation is cancelled after the closing date, you should inform businesses within 10 calendar days of the cancellation.
