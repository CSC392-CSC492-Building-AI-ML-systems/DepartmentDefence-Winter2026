## Technical data

For technical data not owned by Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC), you must ensure that the government obtains rights for its distribution and use.

If the necessary technical data is available through normal business channels, such as Canadian Standards Association (CSA) specifications or standards, businesses are responsible for obtaining it themselves.

If technical data needs to be sent to businesses from an alternate source, such as the client, the solicitation must not be posted until this data is available from that source. The solicitation must clearly identify how to obtain the data.

PSPC buyers working on Department of National Defence requirements must send the completed form PWGSC-TPSGC 1065 - Request for distribution of technical data to:

Directorate, Supply Chain Operations (DSCO) repository section, staff DSCO 4-7 National Printing Bureau (NPB) 45 Blvd. Sacré-Coeur Gatineau, QC, J8X 1C6

You must allow sufficient time to ensure the data will be available when the solicitation of offers is issued.
