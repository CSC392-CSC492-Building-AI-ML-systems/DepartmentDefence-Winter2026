## Exchange rate fluctuation

For contracts with an exchange rate fluctuation provision, you and the client must ensure that this provision is applied during invoicing and payment.

Invoices must indicate the adjustment required and typically include the PSPC Claim for exchange rate adjustments form showing the adjustment calculations, all in line with the contract terms.
