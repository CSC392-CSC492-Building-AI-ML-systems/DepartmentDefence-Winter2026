## Overview of key pricing considerations

To support effective procurement, you should consider:

- managing pricing throughout the acquisition lifecycle
- engaging expert advice, as needed
- documenting and justifying all key pricing decisions and then incorporating them in the contract
- developing a validation strategy
- capturing and tracking lessons learned

See the section 3 of the Practitioner's Guide for Procurement Pricing for more information.
