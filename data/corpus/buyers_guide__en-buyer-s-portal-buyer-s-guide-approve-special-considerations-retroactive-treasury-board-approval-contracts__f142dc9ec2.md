Retroactive Treasury Board approval of contracts Treasury Board (TB) ministers will consider submissions requesting retroactive approval of a contract or contract amendment under exceptional circumstances only. Examples of this include urgent cases related to public safety or security, incidents or cost overruns. TB requires certification that the minister concerned agreed to the commencement of work before obtaining TB approval, including a full report on the reasons, the managerial responsibilities involved and corrective actions taken.
