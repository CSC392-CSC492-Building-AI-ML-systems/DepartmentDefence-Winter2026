## Invitation to tender

Use an Invitation to tender (ITT) when selection is based on the lowest price. In addition, all the following criteria must apply:

- two or more sources are capable of carrying out the requirement
- the requirement is adequately defined to permit an evaluation of offers against clearly stated criteria
- the market conditions are such that offers can be submitted on a common pricing basis
- the intention is to accept the lowest-priced responsive offer without negotiations
- the evaluation of offers will exclude any product, resource, operating and contingency costs or socio-economic considerations, other than the employment equity provisions

Before issuing an ITT , you must verify the Ineligibility and Suspension List to ensure that the invited offerors are not ineligible, if they have not been selected from a prequalified list. The ITT must include the Ineligibility and Suspension Policy Clauses. You can announce an ITT :

- through public advertisement on GETS
- through direct invitation through a source list
- by invitation to one source only if conditions for a non-competitive process have been met

An ITT can be opened publicly and is recommended for requirements estimated to exceed $25,000. For ITT s below $25,000, public opening may be held if circumstances warrant. You should consider public openings for any bid where the contract award will have significant public visibility.
