## Special considerations

Here are some special circumstances to consider during the evaluation process.

### One responsive offer

When only one responsive offer is received in response to a competitive solicitation, you must ensure that Canada is obtaining fair value by following the guidelines below.

#### Offer is greater than $7,500,000

You must contact a cost analyst to determine if further analysis is warranted as specified by the mandatory conditions set out in the Directive on the Use of Cost and Price Analysis Services (accessible only on the Government of Canada network).

Once you have determined the offer represents fair value to Canada, the contract may be awarded using competitive authorities to the single responsive offeror. If you are not satisfied the offer represents fair value, you may consider negotiating with the offeror or cancelling and reissuing the solicitation.

#### Offer is less than or equal to $7,500,000

You should consider using the services of a cost analyst to determine if further analysis is required as specified by the mandatory conditions set out in the Guideline on the Use of Cost and Price Analysis Services (accessible only on the Government of Canada network).

Once you have determined the offer provides fair value to Canada, the contract may be awarded using competitive authorities. If you are not satisfied that the offer represents fair value, you may consider negotiating or cancelling and reissuing the solicitation.

#### One responsive offer from a foreign contractor

When the offer is from an offeror in a North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) allied country (or a significant portion is being proposed to be subcontracted to an offeror in a NATO -allied country), you should consider using the services of the foreign offeror’s country’s government to conduct an assessment of the fairness and reasonableness of the offer.

This service can be attained by submitting a request through PSPC ’s Price Support Directorate or consulting the guidance for price certifications and audits of foreign offerors.

### Evaluate joint venture offers

Joint ventures are permitted to submit offers under the stipulations outlined in the solicitation document.

The evaluation of such offers includes verifying that all members of the joint venture are identified and will be individually liable for the performance of the contract. If a financial capability assessment is done, then all members of the joint venture will be assessed.

#### Financial capability assessment

For joint ventures, a comprehensive financial capability assessment of each member is conducted to:

- ensure each member brings adequate financial stability and resources to the joint venture
- evaluate the collective ability of the joint venture to manage the financial demands of the contract

### Offer evaluation of special program considerations

When evaluating offers consideration must be given to programs such as Canadian content, green procurement, accessible procurement and the federal Contractors Program for employment equity.
