Department of National Defence
Directive on Remote and Hybrid Work

1. Purpose
This directive establishes expectations for remote and hybrid work arrangements within
the Department of National Defence.

2. Applicability
This directive applies to civilian employees of the Department of National Defence.
Canadian Armed Forces members are excluded unless otherwise specified.

3. Eligibility
Remote work arrangements may be approved when operational requirements permit and
when employee duties can be performed effectively outside the workplace.

4. Approval Authority
Managers are responsible for approving remote work arrangements. Approval decisions
must consider security, performance, and operational needs.

5. Relationship to Travel
Remote work should be considered as an alternative to travel where feasible. However,
approved remote work arrangements do not eliminate the requirement for travel when
operational needs require in-person presence.

6. Review
Remote work arrangements must be reviewed periodically and may be revoked at any time
based on operational requirements.
