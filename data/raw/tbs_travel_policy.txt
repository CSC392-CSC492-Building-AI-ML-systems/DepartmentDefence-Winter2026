Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat
Directive on Travel, Hospitality, Conference and Event Expenditures

1. Purpose
This directive establishes requirements for the management of travel, hospitality,
conference and event expenditures across federal departments to ensure value for money
and accountability.

2. Application
This directive applies to all federal departments and agencies subject to the Financial
Administration Act, including the Department of National Defence.

3. Travel Authorization
All travel must be pre-authorized prior to departure. Travel requests must demonstrate
that the travel is necessary to support departmental objectives and that no suitable
virtual alternative is available.

4. Approval Authority
Managers with delegated financial authority are responsible for approving travel
requests. Approval must occur before any travel-related expenses are incurred.

5. Economy Class Travel
Employees must travel using the most economical means available, normally economy
class airfare. Exceptions require documented justification and prior approval.

6. Compliance
Failure to comply with this directive may result in administrative or disciplinary action.
