Department of National Defence
Defence Administrative Order and Directive (DAOD)
Travel and Temporary Duty

1. Scope
This directive provides additional travel guidance specific to the Department of National
Defence and Canadian Armed Forces members.

2. Relationship to Treasury Board Policy
This directive must be read in conjunction with applicable Treasury Board directives.
Where inconsistencies arise, Treasury Board policy takes precedence.

3. Travel Approval
All DND travel requires approval by the appropriate chain of command in accordance with
delegated authorities. Travel must support operational or administrative requirements.

4. Class of Travel
DND personnel are expected to travel economy class. Business class travel may be
authorized only when operational requirements necessitate rest or readiness
considerations.

5. Operational Travel
For operational deployments, expedited approval processes may be used when advance
authorization is not feasible.

6. Record Keeping
All travel approvals and justifications must be documented and retained for audit
purposes.
